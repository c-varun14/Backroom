# What I think we need

From Mira, owner, dictated after closing on 5 November 2026.

We are a tiny bakery and I am tired of finding someone's order between a photo of a dog and seventeen messages about parking. People message me, I promise them a loaf, then forget whether they wanted Saturday or Sunday. I want a link in our profile: see what we bake, choose a pickup day, order and get a confirmation. It should feel like a shop, not a survey.

My cousin says blockchain makes orders impossible to lose. Can you put the orders on that? He also says Kubernetes means we can grow without rebuilding everything. I don't know what either costs. Perhaps start with those now so we are future proof. We should have a mobile app too, because everyone orders on their phone. I don't mean I want to manage two shops; just tap a thing and order. If you have a simpler way, explain it without a diagram full of boxes.

Collection is Thursday through Sunday, nine until eleven in the morning. That is what the paper sign says. People sometimes come after work and I do hand things over, but I do not want to advertise that as a promise. Let customers order until midnight for the following morning? The website should not make people ring us. I bake early and don't check messages before starting. We need the numbers in time to weigh the dough, which is the part everybody forgets when they suggest instant ordering.

For the first menu there are three things: a seed loaf at ₹180, a cinnamon knot at ₹90 and a cocoa cookie box at ₹240. A box contains four cookies and counts as one menu item when somebody chooses a quantity. No substitutions, custom cakes or delivery yet. Collect payment at the counter. Please don't add a payment screen that makes this take another week. Show the total before they confirm so nobody arrives surprised. The confirmation should have a short reference we can read aloud, their date and their basket.

I like cream paper, dark brown lettering and an orange accent. The name is Morrowcrumb Bakery, not Morrow Crumb Cafe. A wordmark and text menu are enough. Please don't borrow photographs from another bakery. I need readable type because my neighbour orders with her glasses off. No account creation, loyalty points or mailing list on the first version.

Ask for a name and email so we can find the order or contact someone if the oven breaks. Don't put customers' names on a public board. I need a private list by collection day, ideally something I can print. Do orders stay there if the computer restarts? Last time a form said success and nothing was actually saved. Success should mean we really have the order. Please tell me what questions I need to answer before you start; I am likely to change my mind if we leave the details fuzzy.
