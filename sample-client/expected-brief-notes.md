# What a good brief preserves

Evaluate against the three initial documents. Do not treat these notes as client approval.

- A responsive browser preorder flow, a three-item menu, basket quantities, pickup date, name and email, displayed total, saved order and private daily pickup list. No account required for customers.
- INR prices: seed loaf 18000 paise, cinnamon knot 9000 paise, cocoa cookie box 24000 paise. No online payment, delivery, discounts, custom products or automatic email in the first version. Show an on-screen reference, basket, date and total after saving.
- Allergen lines visible on EVERY product, including on phones: wheat/sesame; wheat/milk/egg; wheat/milk/egg/soy respectively. Shared kitchen handles nuts. Client must confirm wording; no safety certification or “free from” claims.
- Order cut-off the evening BEFORE pickup, enforced on the server at submission. Proposed 20:00 Asia/Kolkata comes from the baker; 18:00 and midnight also appear. Never silently choose one or equate document recency with approval.
- Daily capacity, probably 40 menu units, not orders or individual cookies. A cookie box consumes one. Separate pickup dates, no overselling when requests race, no silent date changes. Confirm units and number with the owner.
- Thursday–Sunday pickup dates within the next seven calendar days. Define that window precisely in the approved brief; suggested rule: today through today + six days in Asia/Kolkata, with today's cut-off already expired. The following day remains unavailable once the previous evening's cut-off is reached.
- Durable accepted orders, honest save-failure behaviour, protection against duplicate submissions, owner-only access to customer details, no public list of names or emails. Full payable prices and totals calculated from a server catalog.
- Cream, dark brown and orange; readable type; wordmark and text menu acceptable. No borrowed bakery imagery.

# Translate and question

Translate “blockchain” into durable, trustworthy order records; “Kubernetes” into a simple deployment and recovery plan; “mobile app” into a phone-friendly link. Explain why those prescribed technologies are unnecessary for this scope. Do not erase the underlying needs or insult the client.

Ask the owner to resolve 09:00–11:00 versus 16:00–18:00 pickup; midnight versus 18:00 versus 20:00 previous-evening cut-off; 40 orders versus 40 units; and allergen wording. Make the seven-day boundary explicit. Morning pickup with a 20:00 cut-off and 40 units is a proposal until the client approves an identified brief version. The chat explicitly postpones two pickup sessions.

A question keeps the current stage open. A suggestion creates another brief version. Approval is for one exact version. If an agent invents a resolution, marks a draft approved, adds payments, exposes the pickup list, or drops allergens/capacity/cut-off, the brief needs correction.
