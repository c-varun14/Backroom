# Timing method and limitations

`timing.json` stores actual wall-clock start/end spans from `python3 rehearsal/clock.py start|end <step>`. First source-discovery timestamp: 2026-11-05T20:01:18+05:30. Initial playbooks, brief and architecture were drafted together before their write batch at 20:04:17, about 179 seconds including discovery; separate drafting times were not captured. Their raw .03/.05/.02 second spans measure file emission only and MUST NOT be presented as full authoring duration.

Build-v1, deployment-v1 and initial revision spans include their checks/failure fixes between the recorded boundaries. The adversarial authoring batch was drafted between the initial revision end and its own write/test batch; this gap is about 95.40 seconds. Its raw .04 second timer again measures command execution only. Initial review thinking before the review timer is unallocated. Review includes final implementation amendments/redeployment and final browser/API/tests. Closeout includes documentation, final scans, cleanup and report preparation. Some time belongs to batched drafting/gaps rather than a separately measured step; do not invent precision or claim a six-stage live workflow duration from these numbers.
